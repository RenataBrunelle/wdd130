Discover our "Lake of Fear" tour, an unparalleled journey into the heart of exhilarating suspense. This tour provides plenty of adrenaline and hair-raising thrills as you navigate serene yet deceptively deep waters, promising a true test of courage against the mysterious abyss. Prepare for an unforgettable adventure where calm meets chaos and only the bold emerge victorious!

Discover our "Sharp Water" tour, a spectacular, high-octane river challenge designed for the true thrill-seeker. This will be a ride with exhilarating, precision-required curves and extremely strong, relentless currents, providing an intense, fast-paced dance with the river's raw power. Strap in for an epic splash-filled experience that demands sharp reflexes and rewards pure excitement.

Discover our "Ferocious race" tour, the ultimate competitive river challenge where teamwork and endurance are key. Here you'll paddle a lot and have a great time with the challenging course and fast-moving obstacles that simulate a true white-water competition. Grab your team and race against the clock and the river itself for bragging rights and an exhilarating, shared victory!
